# Getting started

From idea to a live pool.

1. DM [@StoaDEX](https://x.com/StoaDEX) with the pair and your price source.
2. Get your maker address approved for the pool and post the bond: 10 HYPE per pool on Elysium, $STOA on Robinhood Chain ([Become a maker](become-a-maker.md)).
3. Deposit starting inventory into your `StoaPool`.
4. Start your maker bot. The [Maker kit](maker-kit.md) is the fastest way.
5. Your pool appears in Pools once quotes are flowing.
