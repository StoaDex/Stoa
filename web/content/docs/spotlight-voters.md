# Spotlight for voters

A short guide to earning ETH with $STOA in Spotlight: lock, vote, check your estimate, claim. The full rules are on [Spotlight](spotlight.md).

![The Your Spotlight panel on stoadex.com/spotlight](/docs/spotlight-your-panel.png)

## 1. Lock $STOA

- Open [stoadex.com/spotlight](https://stoadex.com/spotlight) on Robinhood Chain and connect your wallet.
- In **Your $STOA**, enter an amount and lock it. 1 locked $STOA is 1 vote.
- No $STOA yet? [Buy $STOA](buy-stoa.md) first. You also need a little ETH on Robinhood Chain for gas (cents).

You can lock any time, also before week 1 opens on Monday 23 November 2026 at 00:00 UTC, so your votes are ready.

## 2. Vote

- Every week runs from Monday 00:00 UTC to the next Monday 00:00 UTC. Projects can bid until Saturday 00:00 UTC; after that the bids are final and only votes move.
- In the race table, put your votes on up to ten tokens. You can change them until the week closes.
- A token needs at least 1,000,000 $STOA of votes to be able to win. Among those, the three largest bids win.

## 3. Check your estimate

The **Your Spotlight** panel at the top of the page shows, for the connected wallet:

- **Locked**: your locked $STOA, and anything you asked to unlock with the time it can be withdrawn.
- **Voted this week**: the $STOA you have put on tokens, and how many votes you have not cast yet.
- **If voting closed now**: for each token you voted for, whether it is winning right now and roughly how much ETH your share would be. Each winning token's bid, minus the 10% that buys and burns $STOA, is split among its voters by votes. It is an estimate: bids and other people's votes move until the week ends.
- **To claim**: ETH from past weeks that is ready to claim.
- **Next step**: the one thing to do now (lock, vote, claim or withdraw).

## 4. Claim

- After a week closes and is settled, your ETH appears under **Your ETH to claim**. Claim one week or several at once.
- Claims never expire. Once you have earned something, the claim panel has a link to share your earnings card on X.

## Reminders

Tap **Add weekly reminders** on the Spotlight page to put two weekly events in your calendar: the last hours to vote (Sunday 20:00 to Monday 00:00 UTC) and a check for ETH to claim (Monday 06:00 UTC). Or open [stoadex.com/cal/spotlight.ics](https://stoadex.com/cal/spotlight.ics).

## Good to know

- Locked $STOA earns nothing by itself: you earn when a token you voted for wins.
- To leave, request an unlock. That $STOA stops voting at once. $STOA that voted this week can be requested from next week on, so it stays locked about seven days at least.
- Without enough votes nobody wins that week: bids are refunded and nobody earns.
- This is not financial advice. Bids, votes and payouts can change every week, and nothing is promised.
