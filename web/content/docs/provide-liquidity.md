# Provide liquidity

Deposit into a pool vault and earn from the fees traders pay.

{% hint style="warning" %}

Public vaults are not open yet. They come after Stoa's maker-quoted pools have makers and volume on Robinhood Chain, and after the vault contracts have had an independent security review, because they hold other people's funds. They will open with deposit caps. Until then, pool inventory comes from each pool's curator. To earn with $STOA today, lock it in Spotlight: see [Earn](/earn).

{% endhint %}

1. Open [Earn](https://stoadex.com/earn) and pick a vault.
2. Enter an amount and confirm the deposit.
3. You receive vault shares. Fees are added to the vault, so each share is worth more over time.
4. Withdraw any time by redeeming your shares.

## Risks

Vault value can fall if the maker takes losses on its inventory in fast markets. Read [Risks and safeguards](risks.md) before depositing.
