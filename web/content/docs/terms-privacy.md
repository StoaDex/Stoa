The terms of use for the Stoa interface and how it handles your data.

## Terms of use

**What Stoa is.** Stoa is a website that helps you send transactions to smart contracts on Elysium. The contracts run on a public blockchain and nobody operating this website can stop, reverse or change a transaction after you sign it. Stoa never holds your funds or your keys.

**Your responsibility.** You decide every transaction and sign it in your own wallet. Check the token addresses, amounts, minimum received and price impact before you sign. Tokens imported by address are not reviewed by Stoa and may be worthless or malicious.

**Risks.** Using DeFi can lose you money: prices move, liquidity can be thin, smart contracts can contain bugs (Stoa's contracts are not audited yet), outside venues such as Uniswap, Aero, Ramses or Liquidity Book can fail, and the Elysium network can halt or reorder transactions. See [Risks and safeguards](risks.md).

**Fees.** Swaps pay a 2 bps protocol fee, plus the fee of any outside venue used and network gas. See [Fees](fees.md).

**Spotlight.** Tokens shown under Spotlight are sponsored: their projects paid for the slot and $STOA holders voted for them. Stoa does not review them. What Spotlight voters receive depends only on what projects bid; nothing is promised, and locking $STOA is at your own risk. See [Spotlight](spotlight.md).

**Rug Cover.** Rug Cover is a smart contract with fixed rules, not insurance, and nobody guarantees a payout. A covered buyer can claim only what the contract's rules allow, from the creator's bond, and only if the creator's own wallet drops below its floor before the bond is released. Other wallets, other sellers and price moves are not covered. Backing Rug Cover with $STOA earns only what creators pay or forfeit, which can be nothing. See [Rug Cover](rug-cover.md).

## Privacy

- **No account and no cookies.** Stoa has no sign-up, sets no tracking cookies and runs no advertising scripts.
- **Visit counts.** Stoa counts visits with Vercel Web Analytics, which uses no cookies: a visitor is counted by a hash of the request that is discarded after 24 hours, so you cannot be followed across days or across sites. It records the page, the site you came from, your country, browser and device type. Wallet addresses, transaction hashes and referral addresses are removed from the page address before anything is sent. On Trade it also counts four steps, without any address, amount or token: a wallet connected, a quote shown, a swap sent and a swap confirmed, with the network's name. That shows where people stop, never who they are. Vercel Speed Insights measures how fast pages load and respond on your device (loading time, layout shifts, tap delay), with the same cleaned page address and no cookies.
- **Stored in your browser only.** If you open a referral link, the referrer's address is kept in your browser's local storage so later swaps credit them. Clearing site data removes it.
- **Blockchain data is public.** Your wallet address and every transaction you sign are visible on Elysium and Robinhood Chain to anyone, permanently. Stoa reads them from the chain to show your balances and swap history.
- **Services that see your requests.** The site is hosted on Vercel, fonts load from Google Fonts, and the app talks to Elysium and Robinhood Chain RPC providers (such as Kinetiq, Alchemy and dRPC). Like any website, these providers see your IP address and the requests your browser makes. If you connect through WalletConnect, its relay sees the connection.
